During my time there, I learned a bit about Python programming laguage but unfortunately I did not get selected for the internship program. However, I didn't give up. I discovered a passion for front-end development. The joy of turning ideas into interactive digital experiences. Whether it's building from scratch or bringing a design to life, I enjoy every step of the development process.

I'm currently seeking new opportunities in any work related to front-end development, whether internship or a job (part-time or full-time). I'm open to work remotely and have flexible hours.
